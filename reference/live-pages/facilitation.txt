# Software design facilitation
 Struggling as a software team to collaborate effectively with stakeholders and fully grasp the intricate context of your software system? Interested in exploring collaborative software design to boost stakeholder engagement and make well-informed design decisions that bridge the gap between stakeholders and development teams? Or perhaps you already recognize the power of collaborative modeling tools like EventStorming, Example Mapping, Business Model Canvas, User Story Mapping, Impact Mapping, Wardley Mapping, and more, and need someone to facilitate?
